# 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges in the telecommunications industry. Understanding why customers leave can help companies improve retention strategies and increase long-term profitability.

This Power BI project explores customer churn patterns by analyzing customer demographics, payment methods, contract types, service engagement, monthly charges, and customer tenure.

The dashboard transforms raw customer data into actionable business insights that support data-driven decision making.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze customer churn behavior.
* Identify the major drivers of churn.
* Measure key business KPIs.
* Discover high-risk customer segments.
* Provide recommendations to improve customer retention.

# 📈 Key Insights

## Payment Method

Electronic check customers have the highest churn rate.

## Monthly Charges

Customers with higher monthly charges are more likely to churn.

## Service Engagement

Lower service engagement levels are associated with increased churn.

## Contract Type

Month-to-month contracts show significantly higher churn than long-term contracts.

## Customer Tenure

Customer churn decreases as customer tenure increases.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

### Encourage Automatic Payments

Offer incentives for customers to switch to automatic payment methods.

### Promote Long-Term Contracts

Provide discounts and loyalty rewards for annual subscriptions.

### Improve Customer Onboarding

Target new customers with engagement campaigns during their first months.

### Retain High-Risk Customers

Develop personalized retention offers for high-charge customers.

### Increase Customer Loyalty

Implement reward programs to improve customer lifetime value.
